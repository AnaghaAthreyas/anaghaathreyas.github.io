## Projects

### Fund P&L Attribution Report
**Tools:** Python, yfinance, pandas, openpyxl | [View on GitHub](https://github.com/AnaghaAthreyas/pnl-attribution)

Built an automated monthly P&L attribution tool for a 14-stock global portfolio. The tool pulls live prices, calculates position-level P&L versus budget and prior month, attributes performance by sector and geography, and generates a formatted 5-sheet Excel management report. Portfolio constructed using institutional criteria: market cap above $100B, daily volume above $500M, 6 sectors, 3 geographies.

### Retail Store Expansion Pro-Forma
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Dollar General Annual Report FY2024

Built a 5-year store-level pro-forma profit and loss statement to evaluate a retail store expansion decision. Modeled net present value, internal rate of return, and payback period across bear, base, and bull scenarios against a 10% hurdle rate. Anchored to real disclosed financials ($259 revenue per square foot, 30.2% gross margin) from Dollar General's FY2024 annual report.

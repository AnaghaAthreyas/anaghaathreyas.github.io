Surplus2Smiles — I founded this

Indian weddings are not small affairs. Hundreds of guests, multiple days, and tables of food that never stop coming. I was at one such wedding when I wandered into the kitchen and asked the chef a simple question: what happens to the leftovers?

His answer stayed with me. At least 20% of all food prepared goes to waste — not because no one wants it, but because no system exists to move it from where it is to where it's needed.

So I built one. Surplus2Smiles connects event organizers to a shelter home housing over 80 residents — people of all ages, all abilities. I visited the home myself, checked their storage, made sure the logistics would actually work, and created a channel that keeps running. Surplus becomes sustenance.

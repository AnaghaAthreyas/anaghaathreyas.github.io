Surplus2Smiles — I founded this

Indian weddings are not small affairs. Hundreds of guests, multiple days, and tables of food that never stop coming. I was at one such wedding when I wandered into the kitchen and asked the chef a simple question: what happens to the leftovers?

His answer stayed with me. At least 20% of all food prepared goes to waste — not because no one wants it, but because no system exists to move it where it's needed. So I built one.

Surplus2Smiles connects event organizers to a shelter home housing over 80 residents of all ages and abilities. I visited the home, verified storage capacity, and created a channel that keeps running. Surplus becomes sustenance.

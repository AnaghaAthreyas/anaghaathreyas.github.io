Surplus2Smiles

Large social gatherings in India routinely generate significant food surplus. At an event I attended, I spoke with the catering team and learned that on average, 20 percent of all food prepared goes to waste — not because of scarcity of need, but because no infrastructure exists to move it from where it is to where it is needed.

In response, I established Surplus2Smiles, an initiative connecting event organisers with a local shelter home housing residents across age groups and abilities. Before formalising any partnership, I visited the facility to assess storage capacity and operational readiness. The channel has since run consistently, redirecting food that would otherwise go to waste to hundreds of people who benefit from it.

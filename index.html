80+ lives nourished

Surplus2Smiles — Founded by Anagha

Indian weddings are grand affairs — hundreds of guests, elaborate spreads, and inevitably, enormous amounts of leftover food. After attending one such wedding, I asked the chef what happens to it all. His answer: at least 20% of all food prepared goes to waste, discarded simply because of its perishable nature.


That conversation became the seed of Surplus2Smiles — a food redistribution initiative connecting event organizers to a shelter home housing over 80 residents of all ages and abilities. I conducted site inspections to verify storage capacity and built a reliable, repeatable channel so surplus becomes sustenance, not landfill.
